Pyronear is a non-profit NGO that democratizes open and low-tech solutions to combat wildfires, benefiting ecosystems and citizens. At Pyronear, we are developing a performant, automatic, modular, economical, and energy-efficient open-source early detection solution.

# How can I contribute?

Our mission is to help create a world where accessible and sustainable technology enables the protection of ecosystems, and we believe that collective intelligence and open collaboration are the solutions.  Whether you are an expert or not, there’s a place for you in the project.

## Spread the word

⭐ Star and watch our repos, visibility helps other volunteers find us.

🔄 [Follow](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pyronearfr/posts/?feedView=all) and share, fighting wildfires is a collective challenge, and you can help us raise awareness.

## Push the code


👩‍💻 Check out our [good first issues](https://github.com/search?q=org%3Apyronear+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22++state%3A%22open%22&type=issues&state=open&p=1), jump to any repositories, and start preserving forests from wildfires one commit at a time.
